Release Date: December 16, 2021

Created by: Patrick Somerville

Starring: Mackenzie Davis, Himesh Patel, Matilda Lawler, David Wilmot

Synopsis

A post-apocalyptic saga spanning multiple timelines, Station Eleven tells the stories of survivors of a devastating flu as they attempt to rebuild and reimagine the world anew while holding on to the best of what's been lost. Twenty years after the collapse of civilization, a group of travelers known as the Traveling Symphony encounters a violent cult whose leader's past is unknowingly linked to one of the troupe's members.
